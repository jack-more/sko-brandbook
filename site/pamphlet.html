Your script

Eleven beats

A 45-second video can use all eleven. A 20-second video uses four or five. The ones marked ALWAYS belong in every video you make.

01Problem
ALWAYS Name the thing your viewer already deals with, in the first two seconds. Use one you actually have — an invented problem reads as an ad instantly.
02Statistic One real number. The only one we give you is 40,000+ orders shipped. If you do not have a number you can stand behind, skip this beat — never invent one.
03FOMO What they are missing while they keep putting it off. Not urgency theatre and not a fake deadline — the actual cost of waiting.
04Self-
identification
ALWAYS The line that makes the right viewer realise this video is for them. "If you're someone who…" is the plain version; yours should sound like you.
05Product
discovery How you came across SKO. This is the first time the brand is named, and it should sound like a story rather than a handoff into an ad read.
06Objection Say their doubt out loud before they think it. Whatever made you hesitate is almost certainly what is making them hesitate.
07Visual proof
ALWAYS Show it. The vial in your hand, the box on your counter, the label, your order screen. Talking about a product you never show is the most common reason these videos do not convert.
08Social
normalization Other people are already doing this. It stops being a risk and starts being a thing people do.
09Credibility
ALWAYS Three facts, in your own words, and only these three: third-party tested, Beverly Hills based, 40,000+ orders shipped. Nothing about purity or percentages.
10Simplicity How easy it actually was to order. Most people assume it is complicated and stop there.
11Call to action
ALWAYS Your code, 10% off, where to go. Say it out loud and put it on screen. You are paid on this beat and it is the one people forget.
Under 25 seconds, use the short version: Problem → Self-identification → Visual proof → Credibility → Call to action.

Week one

Five videos, five styles

Most creator programs hand you a script and then wonder why the videos feel dead. Yours will not, because you know your audience and we do not. Make one of each, in your own editing style, then we keep whatever worked. Do not make the same video five times with different backgrounds.

STYLE ONE

Talking to camera

You, your room, no edit tricks. Not the lazy option — our best-performing videos so far are exactly this, one person talking straight into the lens.

STYLE TWO

In hand / unboxing

The box arrives, you open it, you read the label out loud. Heavy on visual proof, and the easiest one to make convincing.

STYLE THREE

In your routine

Where it actually sits in your day. Shot like the rest of your day-in-the-life content, not like a commercial.

STYLE FOUR

Street or reaction

You asking someone else what they think, or reacting to something on screen. Works when the other person's reaction is real.

STYLE FIVE

Your own format

Whatever already performs on your page, with the beats built inside it. Honestly, this is the one we are most interested in — if it works we would rather learn it than standardise it away.

Hard rules

Not style preferences

Breaking one of these can cost you your account or cost us our ad account. If you are unsure whether something crosses a line, ask in the group before you post, not after.

NEVER

Say what to take, how much, or what it does to a body. No dosing, no protocols, no "I take this for that." These are research compounds, and the moment a video sounds like medical advice it is a problem for you and for us.

NEVER

Post before-and-afters. No body transformation shots and no results claims.

NEVER

Use weight-loss language — spoken, on screen, or in the caption. That includes every brand name in that category.

NEVER

Make purity claims. Not a percentage, not "tested eight times." The only trust line is third-party tested. The number printed on the physical label is part of the product and can be visible on camera — just do not read it out or put it in your caption.

NEVER

Buy comments or use engagement groups. It is against TikTok's rules and it is the fastest way to lose a page you spent years building.

ALWAYS

Leave TikTok's AI label on if you used AI anywhere in the edit. Removing it is how accounts get actioned.

ALWAYS

Replace a taken-down video within 48 hours, and tell us in the group what the notice said, so everyone else can avoid it.
