Week one is a test

03 · FIVE VIDEOS, FIVE SHAPES

Most creator programs hand you a script and then wonder why the videos feel dead. Yours will not, because you know your audience and we do not. So in week one you make five videos in five different shapes — all in your own editing style, all built on the beats below.

Then we look at what actually happened and you spend the rest of the month making more of whatever won. If one shape clearly outperforms, that becomes your format. If nothing does, we change the approach rather than asking you to try harder.

One rule for week one: do not make the same video five times with different backgrounds. The point is five genuinely different attempts.

The eleven beats

04 · A MENU, NOT A CHECKLIST

A 45-second video can use all eleven. A 20-second video uses four or five. The ones marked CORE belong in every video you make.

  1. 01

    ProblemCORE

    Name the thing your viewer already deals with, in the first two seconds. Use one you actually have — an invented problem reads as an ad instantly.

  2. 02

    Statistic

    One real number. The only one we have given you is that SKO has shipped 40,000+ orders. If you do not have a number you can stand behind, skip this beat — never invent one.

  3. 03

    FOMO

    What they are missing while they keep putting it off. Not urgency theatre and not a fake deadline — the actual cost of waiting.

  4. 04

    Self-identificationCORE

    The line that makes the right viewer realise this video is for them. "If you're someone who…" is the plain version; yours should sound like you.

  5. 05

    Product discovery

    How you came across SKO. This is the first time the brand is named, and it should sound like a story rather than a handoff into an ad read.

  6. 06

    Objection

    Say their doubt out loud before they think it. Whatever made you hesitate is almost certainly what is making them hesitate.

  7. 07

    Visual proofCORE

    Show it. The vial in your hand, the box on your counter, the label, your order screen. Talking about a product you never show is the most common reason these videos do not convert.

  8. 08

    Social normalization

    Other people are already doing this. It stops being a risk and starts being a thing people do.

  9. 09

    CredibilityCORE

    Three facts, in your own words, and only these three: third-party tested, Beverly Hills based, 40,000+ orders shipped. Nothing about purity, percentages, or how many times anything was tested.

  10. 10

    Simplicity

    How easy it actually was to order. Most people assume it is complicated and stop there.

  11. 11

    Call to actionCORE

    Your code, 10% off, where to go. Say it out loud and put it on screen. You are paid on this beat and it is the one people forget.

Under 25 seconds, use the short version: Problem → Self-identification → Visual proof → Credibility → Call to action.

Five shapes

05 · ONE EACH, ANY ORDER
SHAPE 01

Talking to camera

You, your room, no edit tricks. Not the lazy option — our best-performing videos so far are exactly this, one person talking straight into the lens.

SHAPE 02

In hand / unboxing

The box arrives, you open it, you read the label out loud. Heavy on beat 07, and the easiest one to make convincing.

SHAPE 03

In your routine

Where it actually sits in your day. Shot like the rest of your day-in-the-life content, not like a commercial.

SHAPE 04

Street or reaction

You asking someone else what they think, or reacting to something on screen. Works when the other person's reaction is real.

SHAPE 05

Your own format

Whatever already performs on your page, with the beats built inside it. Honestly, this is the one we are most interested in.

Hard rules

07 · NOT STYLE PREFERENCES

Breaking one of these can cost you your account or cost us our ad account. If you are unsure whether something crosses a line, ask in the group before you post, not after.

  • NEVER

    Say what to take, how much, or what it does to a body. No dosing, no protocols, no "I take this for that." These are research compounds, and the moment a video sounds like medical advice it is a problem for you and for us.

  • NEVER

    Post before-and-afters. No body transformation shots and no results claims.

  • NEVER

    Use weight-loss language — spoken, on screen, or in the caption. That includes every brand name in that category.

  • NEVER

    Make purity claims. Not a percentage, not "tested eight times." The only trust line is third-party tested. The number printed on the physical label is part of the product and can be visible on camera — just do not read it out or put it in your caption.

  • NEVER

    Buy comments or use engagement groups. It is against TikTok's rules and it is the fastest way to lose a page you spent years building.

  • ALWAYS

    Leave TikTok's AI label on if you used AI anywhere in the edit. Removing it is how accounts get actioned.

  • ALWAYS

    Replace a taken-down video within 48 hours, and tell us in the group what the notice said, so everyone else can avoid it.
