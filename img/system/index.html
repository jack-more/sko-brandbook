Ground rule. The full-colour master lockup goes on white only. Its wordmark is dark blue, so on any coloured ground it loses itself. On blue, on ink, on photography, on any colour at all — use the white logo. No exceptions, and never place the full-colour mark on the brand blue.
The field. Yves Klein Blue is #002FA7, hue 223. SKO Blue is #173384, hue 224 — the same hue, ours darker. The brand already owns the colour, so there is nothing to chase. What the reference gives us is a usage rule: Klein’s blue was never a backdrop, it was the work. So when our blue runs large it runs flat, edge to edge, with nothing behind it and no gradient inside it, and one machined object sits in it. Field #244399 is the value for that — the same hue held brighter, so it carries a whole surface without going black. Blue against polished steel is the whole composition; the chrome mark, the crimp collar and the foil band are the steel.

The field

Blue used flat, edge to edge, with one machined object in it. This is the Klein rule, and it is the only way the blue runs large.
