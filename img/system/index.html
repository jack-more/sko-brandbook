SKO — Brand system

The mark, explored. Bands are straight; the angle belongs to camera moves around the logo and the bottles, not to a static layout. The shield is woven from double helices — the emblem takes the treatment, the wordmark stays solid. Blue #244399, white, Halo #9DBFD9.

The band
